Research

Design and Evaluation of a Modular Multi-Contract Blockchain Framework for Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Traceability

Accepted for oral presentation at ICTDsC 2026 (Paper ID 162, scheduled for November 19–20, 2026). Authors: Suryanshu Verma, Shreejal Bista, Suman Yadav, Subarna Shakya.

Pharmaceutical supply chains often depend on a single authority for manufacturing, shipment, and verification records, which leaves those records open to tampering. The paper proposes an Ethereum framework split into three smart contracts — UserRegistry, BatchRegistry and ProductRegistry — for stakeholder authorization, batch lifecycle tracking, and product lookup, storing only integrity-sensitive fields on-chain to limit gas cost.

  • Public testnet (Ethereum Sepolia): 50 / 50 benchmarked transactions confirmed; confirmation times 7.03–24.57 s.
  • Local benchmark (Hardhat): under 4 ms mean latency per core operation; 179.08 tx/s at 2,000 batches; 8 / 8 security validation tests passed.

Implementation: PharmaTrace on GitHub.
